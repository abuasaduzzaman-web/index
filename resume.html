Abu Asaduzzaman

Professor of Computer Engineering

Abu Asaduzzaman is currently a full professor and serves as the Associate Chair of his department. Dr. Asaduzzaman’s research interests include high-performance computing, machine learning, and cybersecurity. He has authored over 150 research articles. He has received research grants from NSF EPSCoR, DoE Argonne National Laboratory, Nvidia, NetApp, and other organizations. Dr. Asaduzzaman reviews for NSF and other programs. He serves for IEEE Regions, Sections, and conferences.
