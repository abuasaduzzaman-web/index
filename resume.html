Abu Asaduzzaman

Professor of Computer Engineering

Dr. Asaduzzaman's research interests include high-performance computing, machine learning, data analytics, and cybersecurity. He has authored over 150 research articles. He has received research grants from NSF EPSCoR, DoE Argonne National Laboratory, Nvidia, NetApp, J3D Labs (FLIP), and other organizations. He is currently the Associate Chair of his department.
